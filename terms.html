Terms

Last updated 2 November 2026

These short terms apply to FreshFrame services provided by Oğuzcan Karaman. A written scope sent before each Pilot or plan takes precedence where it differs.

Pilot

$490, one-off. Five videos for one product area, delivered within five business days after we receive a demo account and the flow list, with one revision round per video.

Starter

$790 per month. Five new videos in the first month, then two new videos each month. Up to 15 videos are kept current: a weekly check of every flow, and re-recording and re-voicing of any video whose screen changed (fair use; a full redesign that rewrites most of the library is scoped separately). One revision round per video. Unused new videos do not carry over. The plan starts on the day you subscribe and renews on that date each month. If you subscribe within 30 days of your Pilot's delivery, the Pilot's five videos are your first-month videos: they are kept current from the first day, the $490 Pilot fee is credited against the first monthly payment (so it is $300), and the two new videos per month start in the second month. After 30 days the credit no longer applies and the plan starts like a new one, with five new videos in the first month.

Billing and cancellation

Prices are in USD, excluding tax. The Pilot is paid upfront. Starter is billed monthly in advance and renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time by email; cancellation takes effect at the end of the month already paid, with no further charges.

Ownership

You own every delivered video and caption file, including after you cancel. We keep our own tooling and templates.

Your demo account

You provide a non-production account with sample data and confirm you may share it with us. We use it only to record and check your flows.

Voice-over

Voice-overs use a synthetic neural voice, not a human recording. Alternative voices from a premium voice library are available on request. You approve each script and video before it is final.

Liability

Services are provided with reasonable care. Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the previous three months.
