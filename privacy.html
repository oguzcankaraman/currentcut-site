Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Currentcut is run by Oğuzcan Karaman. This page explains what we collect and why. Questions: oguz@currentcut.app.

What we collect

When you book a call (Cal.com) or fill in a form (Tally), we receive what you enter: your name, work email, company, product URL and your answers. This site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Why

Only to reply to you, scope and deliver your project, and send approval requests for your videos. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Demo-account credentials

If you become a customer, you give us access to a demo or sandbox account, never a production account with real customer data. Credentials are kept in an encrypted password manager, used only to record and check your flows, and deleted when you cancel.

Processors

Cal.com (scheduling), Tally (forms), Google (email), GitHub Pages (hosting this site). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Retention and your rights

Enquiries that do not turn into a project are deleted within 12 months. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data by emailing the address above.
