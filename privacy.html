Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Who is responsible

Currentcut is a trade name used by Oğuzcan Karaman, a private individual resident in Çankaya, Ankara, Türkiye (not yet a registered company). I am the controller of the personal data described here. Questions or requests: oguz@currentcut.app.

What I collect

What you enter when you fill in a form (Tally) or book a call (Cal.com): name, work email, company, product URL and your answers. Emails and calls with me. This site only counts page views with GoatCounter, which is cookieless and builds no personal profiles.

Why, and on what basis

To answer you and take steps before a contract at your request, and to deliver and bill a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5/2(c)). For B2B outreach and basic site statistics I rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5/2(f)).

If I emailed you first

If I emailed you first, I found your work address on a public page, or worked it out from the address format your company publishes (for example firstname@company.com), sometimes with the help of Hunter. I send a few personal emails, not a newsletter, and stop when you say so. I do not sell or share your data.

Who processes data for me

Cal.com (scheduling), Tally (forms), Zoho Mail (email, EU data centre), GitHub Pages (hosting this site), GoatCounter (page counts), Hunter (finding and checking public work email addresses), Lemon Squeezy (payments, as merchant of record) and Bitwarden (credentials). Some of them are outside the EU/EEA and Türkiye, such as the US; where that applies they rely on their own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Each has its own privacy policy.

Demo-account credentials

If you become a customer, you give me a demo or sandbox account, never a production account with real customer data. Credentials are kept in an encrypted password manager, used only to record and check your flows, and deleted within 30 days after the engagement ends. For this data you are the controller and I am your processor; a data processing agreement (GDPR Art. 28) is available on request, and the terms include the short version.

How long I keep it

Enquiries and outreach that do not turn into a project are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept as long as the engagement lasts, plus what tax and accounting rules require for invoices.

Your rights

You can ask me to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it (object). Email the address above. You can also complain to your supervisory authority, or to the KVKK Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) in Türkiye.
