The demo

Same video. Old UI, new UI. Under ten minutes.

First, a tutorial recorded against an earlier version of an app. Then the same video after the interface changed and FreshFrame regenerated it: new screens, new voice-over, no manual work.

The problem

Every release makes your help center a little more wrong.

A few articles have a video, recorded once, back when the screen looked different. Then the navigation moved and a button got renamed. Now the video shows a product that no longer exists, and nobody wants to re-record thirty videos every sprint.

Videos go stale quietly

Nobody notices until a customer writes in: “that’s not what I see.”

Re-recording is nobody’s job

Hours of scripting, recording, voice-over and editing, out of date again next month.

Capture tools half-solve it

Step captures are quick to make, then go stale the same way. You still have to find each one.

How it works

Three steps. None of them on your side.

  1. 01 · record

    We script and record your flows

    You give us a demo account and the tasks users struggle with. We write each script from your help-center wording, you approve it, and we produce a 50–90 second video: your app in a clean window, smooth camera, voice-over synced to every click.

  2. 02 · check

    We check your UI every week

    An automated check replays each flow against your live product and compares every step with the last approved version. Unchanged flows are left alone; noise such as timestamps is masked.

  3. 03 · refresh

    We re-make it, you approve

    When a screen differs, the affected video is re-recorded and re-voiced. You approve it or request a change in a one-minute form, and we deliver the new file.

Questions

Questions you should ask

Is the voice-over a real person?

No. It is a natural-sounding neural voice reading the script you approved, paced to the screen. You hear it on your first video before we produce the rest. If you want a different voice, accent or tone, we can switch to a premium voice library on request; ask on the call.

Our UI changes every week. Will this keep up?

That is the case it is built for. Each week the check replays every flow and compares the screens with the approved version. Only flows where a screen actually changed are re-recorded. If a change breaks a flow outright (a button was removed), we flag it and adjust the script rather than guess.

We already use Loom, Scribe or Arcade. Why this?

Those tools are great for quick captures, and they leave the maintenance with you: someone has to notice what changed and redo it. FreshFrame is a service that produces the videos and keeps them current. If your existing videos are still fine, keep them.

Do you need a login to our product?

Yes, to record. We ask for a dedicated demo or sandbox workspace with sample data, never a real customer account, ideally with a role that cannot delete or bill anything.

How do you handle data security?

We only see what is in the demo account you give us, so keep real customer data out of it. Credentials are kept in an encrypted password manager, used only to record and check your flows, and deleted when you cancel.

Which languages?

English at launch. Other languages for voice-over and captions are possible; ask on the call and we will tell you whether we can do it well.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Every delivered MP4 and caption file is yours to host and keep, including after you cancel.

What do you need from us each week?

Nothing, unless a video changed. Then you get a short approval request: approve, or tell us what to fix with a timestamp and a comment.
