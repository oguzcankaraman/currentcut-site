Guidde, Scribe or Clueso alternative: managed help-center videos

By Oğuzcan Karaman · Updated Nov 12, 2026 · Based on each vendor's public website on that date; check their current features.

Guidde, Scribe and Clueso are good tools, and for many teams they are the right answer. This page explains where a managed service like Currentcut fits instead, so you can pick the one that matches how your team works.

Tools versus a service

All three are software your team operates. Someone on your side captures or uploads a flow, reviews the output, and comes back to redo it when the product changes. Currentcut is a service: we record the videos on a demo account, and we re-record and re-voice them when your UI changes. Your part is to approve.

When a tool is the better choice

  • You have someone with time to own videos and want full control of every edit.
  • You need many internal how-tos or SOPs, not customer-facing help-center videos.
  • You want to create videos yourself the moment a feature ships.

When a managed service fits

  • Nobody owns help-center videos, and the ones you have are a year or more old.
  • Your product ships UI changes often and the team will not re-record each time.
  • You want a consistent look and voice across all videos without building the skill in-house.
  • You would rather review a finished video than operate another tool.

How to decide in 10 minutes

  1. Count the videos in your help center and note the oldest. See the audit checklist.
  2. Ask who will redo them after the next redesign. If you can name a person with time, try a tool. If not, a service removes the gap.
  3. Try each option on the same single flow and compare the result and the effort on your side.
