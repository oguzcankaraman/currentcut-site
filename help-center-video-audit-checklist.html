Help-center video audit checklist: how to find outdated videos

By Oğuzcan Karaman · Updated Nov 12, 2026

You can audit a help center's videos in about 30 minutes for a product with 20 to 40 videos. The goal is not a perfect inventory, it is a ranked list of videos that show a UI your customers no longer see.

Step 1: list every video

Search your help center for embeds (YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Loom, Guidde) and your own video channel. For each video record the items in the table below. A spreadsheet is enough.

ColumnWhy
Title and linkIdentifies the video.
Publish dateThe first age signal. Check the player, not the article date: articles are often edited while the video stays the same.
Flow shownThe product area the video covers (for example "invite a user").
Articles that embed itTells you how many pages a fix improves.
Page trafficDecides priority.

Step 2: cross-check with your changelog

Open your changelog or release notes. For each video, ask whether its product area changed after the publish date: a redesign, a renamed section, a new required step. A video from before a redesign of the screen it shows is outdated by definition. Mark it.

No changelog? Use the video's age as a fallback: anything older than 12 to 18 months in a product that ships regularly deserves a look.

Step 3: watch flagged videos next to the live product

Play the video while the product is open on a second screen and follow it click by click. Note the first moment they differ. Typical findings:

  • A menu item has moved, been renamed or is gone.
  • A step appears that the video does not mention.
  • The colors, logo or layout look clearly like an older version.
  • The narration names a plan, number or feature that changed.

Step 4: rank and decide

  1. Fix first: videos on onboarding and "getting started" pages, and any video whose steps no longer work.
  2. Fix next: videos with visibly old layouts on high-traffic articles.
  3. Leave or remove: low-traffic videos with cosmetic differences only. Remove any video that is wrong and cannot be fixed soon.

Step 5: make it a routine

An audit is a snapshot. To stop the list growing again, add a check to your release process: when a release changes a screen, look up which videos show it. See how to keep help-center videos up to date for the process.
